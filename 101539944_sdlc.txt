1. What is the SDLC?
The SDLC is a structured process teams follow to plan, build, test, release
and maintain software. It helps control cost, quality and time.

2. Phases
1. Planning: decide goals, scope, budget and schedule.
2. Requirements analysis: gather what users and the business need.
3. Design: plan the architecture, database and user interface.
4. Implementation: write the code.
5. Testing: find and fix bugs, check requirements are met.
6. Deployment: release the software to users.
7. Maintenance: fix issues, improve and update over time.


3. Common models
- Waterfall: phases happen in strict order. Simple and well documented, but
  hard to change once a phase is finished.
- Agile: work is done in short cycles (sprints) with frequent feedback.
  Flexible, but needs close communication with the customer.
- Spiral: repeats cycles with a focus on risk analysis.
- V-Model: each development phase is paired with a testing phase.
