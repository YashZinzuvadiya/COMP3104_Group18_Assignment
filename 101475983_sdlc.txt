Importance of DevOps in the SDLC
================================

DevOps joins development and operations so that software moves from planning to production faster and with fewer errors. It removes the hand-off gaps of traditional SDLC models.

Continuous integration runs builds and tests on every push, so bugs are found in the coding and testing phases rather than after release.

Continuous delivery and deployment automate releases, making them repeatable and low risk. Monitoring and feedback in operations then feed back into planning.

Version control gives the team history, traceability and safe collaboration through branches and pull requests, which supports code review and quality.
