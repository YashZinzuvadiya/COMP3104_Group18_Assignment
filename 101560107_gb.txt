CAMPUSES

George Brown has three main campuses, and all of them are in downtown
Toronto. They are the St. James campus, the Casa Loma campus and the
Waterfront campus.

The St. James campus is a big hub for programs in business, media,
design, social services and culinary arts. The Casa Loma campus is one
of the original campuses from when the college was first formed in
1967. The Waterfront campus is close to Lake Ontario and has a newer
building called Limberlost Place. It is made of mass timber, which is
a low-carbon building material, and it is home to the School of
Computer Technology.

Having the campuses in the downtown core is a big advantage for
students. We are close to many employers, so it is easier to find
placements and make industry connections while studying. It is also
easy to get to the campuses by public transit.


PROGRAMS AND SCHOOLS

George Brown offers around 175 full-time programs, including about 10
degree programs. The programs are career focused, and every career program
includes at least one experiential learning opportunity, like a work
placement, so students get real experience. I study in the School of
Computer Technology, in the Computer Programming and Analysis program.


STUDENT LIFE AND FACILITIES

George Brown has services that help students during their studies.
Counselling is free and confidential for registered students. There is
also a student residence called The George, which is within walking
distance of the St. James and Waterfront campuses. Students can also
read The Dialog, the student newspaper run by the Student Association.
