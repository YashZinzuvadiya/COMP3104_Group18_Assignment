1. About the college
George Brown College is a public college in Toronto, Ontario. It offers
diplomas, certificates, advanced diplomas, degrees and graduate certificates,
with a strong focus on hands-on, career-oriented learning.


2. Campuses
- St. James Campus: the main downtown campus, home to business, technology
  and many other programs.
- Casa Loma Campus: located near Casa Loma, with programs in areas such as
  technology, health sciences and community services.
- Waterfront Campus: located on Toronto's waterfront, with programs in areas
  such as health, community and human services.
(Check gbc.ca to confirm the current campus list and which programs run where.)


3. Programs
- Technology: Computer Programmer Analyst, Computer Systems Technology,
  Information Technology related programs.
- Business: accounting, marketing and management programs.
- Health sciences and community services.
- Arts and design, and hospitality and culinary programs.

4. Student life and support
- Student clubs, events and an active student association.
- Career services, co-op and work-integrated learning opportunities.
- Libraries, labs, tutoring and academic support services.
